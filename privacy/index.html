Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information LinkedFiles collects when you use this website and the LinkedFiles desktop application.

Website

This marketing site is static HTML and CSS. It uses two third-party services that run in your browser: PostHog for analytics and the TikTok Pixel for advertising measurement.

Analytics (PostHog, US servers). PostHog records which pages are visited, how long, which site referred you, your approximate country, browser and device type, and whether you click a Download button. It runs without cookies or other storage in your browser, so those visits aren’t linked across days, and it doesn’t record your screen, keystrokes or other clicks.

Advertising (TikTok Pixel). We advertise LinkedFiles on TikTok. The TikTok Pixel tells TikTok when a page on this site is viewed, so we can see how many visits come from those ads and so TikTok can show LinkedFiles ads to people who visited. TikTok receives the page URL, the referring page, the time of the visit, your IP address, browser and device information, and an identifier stored in a TikTok cookie (such as _ttp). TikTok may link this to your TikTok account if you have one. We do not send TikTok your name, email address, phone number, or anything from the desktop application. TikTok handles this data under its Partner Privacy Policy.

Under some US state privacy laws, letting an advertising service collect this data can count as “sharing” personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money.

Hosting (Vercel). The site is hosted on Vercel, which may log IP addresses, request URLs and browser identifiers in server logs. See Vercel’s privacy policy for details.

Contact. Contact requests sent through the Contact page or by email are handled by LinkedFiles. We use your message only to respond to your request.

Cookies and your choices

The only cookies this site’s scripts set are TikTok’s. PostHog and the site itself set none. You can limit TikTok tracking by:

  • Blocking or deleting third-party and tracking cookies in your browser settings, or using a content blocker
  • Turning off personalized ads in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, then Ads
  • Contacting us to ask about, correct, or delete information related to your visits

Blocking cookies does not stop you from using this site or downloading LinkedFiles.

Desktop application

Project files, file names, paths, project names, and search results are never uploaded. Search and the local index stay on your computer.

The app goes online only to check GitHub for updates, to open blender.org when you click Download Blender, and to send anonymous usage statistics to PostHog (US servers).

Anonymous usage statistics are on by default. The first launch explains them and offers OK or Turn Off. You can switch them any time with Share anonymous usage stats in the Blender menu.

Those statistics contain only event names, with counts and the app and Blender versions, plus a random install ID that is not linked to a person. Event names cover app opened, search finished or failed, relink, move, or delete done, failed, or undone, update installed, and feedback opened. They do not include clicks, screens, crash reports, file names, or paths.

Settings are stored in %APPDATA%\com.linkedfiles.app. The search cache, log, backups, and operation records are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.linkedfiles.app.

Children’s privacy

LinkedFiles is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do.
